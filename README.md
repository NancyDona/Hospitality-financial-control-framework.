# Hospitality Financial Control & Internal Audit Framework

## Executive Summary
This project outlines a comprehensive business process and financial control structure designed for mid-sized hospitality enterprises[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). It addresses revenue leakage, inventory discrepancies, and departmental reconciliation gaps by establishing strict digital payment policies and structured auditing workflows[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

---

## Core Mission & Objectives
* **Mission:** Maintain absolute operational integrity through transparent, cashless financial processes, standardized logging, and zero-tolerance revenue leakage across all operating departments[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
* **100% Cashless Policy:** Eliminate unrecorded physical cash transactions across all revenue centers[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
* **Inventory Control:** Reduce unverified bar/kitchen inventory losses (breakage/spoilage) to under **1.0%** of total F&B gross revenue[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
* **Audit Reconciliation:** Attain a **net-zero variance** between physical bank/POS credit logs and recorded departmental sales during every audit cycle[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
* **Standardization:** Standardize 100% of inter-departmental postings[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

---

## Operational Architecture & Process Flow
The framework governs four key functional areas[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span):

1. **Front Desk:** Guest check-in/out, room allocations, and rate verification[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
2. **Food & Beverage (F&B):** Sales management across Kitchen, VIP Bar, and Outdoor Bar[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
3. **Housekeeping:** Property occupancy monitoring and independent physical room drops[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
4. **Audit & Internal Control:** Account reconciliation, ledger matching, and bank log verification[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
