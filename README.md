# Hospitality Financial Control & Internal Audit Framework

## Executive Summary
This project outlines a comprehensive business process and financial control structure designed for mid-sized hospitality enterprises. It addresses revenue leakage, inventory discrepancies, and departmental reconciliation gaps by establishing strict digital payment policies and structured auditing workflows.

---

## Core Mission & Objectives
* **Mission:** Maintain absolute operational integrity through transparent, cashless financial processes, standardized logging, and zero-tolerance revenue leakage across all operating departments.
* **100% Cashless Policy:** Eliminate unrecorded physical cash transactions across all revenue centers.
* **Inventory Control:** Reduce unverified bar/kitchen inventory losses (breakage/spoilage) to under **1.0%** of total F&B gross revenue.
* **Audit Reconciliation:** Attain a **net-zero variance** between physical bank/POS credit logs and recorded departmental sales during every audit cycle.
* **Standardization:** Standardize 100% of inter-departmental postings.

---

## Operational Architecture & Process Flow
The framework governs four key functional areas:

1. **Front Desk:** Guest check-in/out, room allocations, and rate verification.
2. **Food & Beverage (F&B):** Sales management across Kitchen, VIP Bar, and Outdoor Bar.
3. **Housekeeping:** Property occupancy monitoring and independent physical room drops.
4. **Audit & Internal Control:** Account reconciliation, ledger matching, and bank log verification.
